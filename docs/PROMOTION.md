# Promotion Kit

## One-Liner

Auto TikTok is an AI short-video autopilot that turns topics into Douyin/TikTok-ready vertical videos with scripts, voiceover, subtitles, covers, quality gates, Dashboard, Docker, and publishing workflows.

## Short Pitch

Auto TikTok helps creators and builders automate the repetitive parts of short-video production. It creates a structured `video_plan.json`, generates scripts and assets with MiniMax, composes videos with FFmpeg, tracks quota and run history in a local Dashboard, and can export publish-ready packages or call TikTok's Content Posting API when configured.

I just open-sourced Auto TikTok: an AI short-video autopilot for Douyin/TikTok workflows.

Built for creators and developers who want a practical self-hosted pipeline instead of a pile of disconnected scripts.
